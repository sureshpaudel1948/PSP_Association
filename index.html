Welcome to the Home of Digital Wallets of Nepal

Payment Service Provider Association

We are an umbrella organisation of Payment Service Providers, also known as Digital Wallets, operating in Nepal under the license of Nepal Rastra Bank. We are an independent, non-partisan, and not-for-profit organisation committed to promoting and developing digital payments through collective efforts. Through advocacy, innovation, and collaboration, we promote digital literacy, policy support, and professional development to drive Nepal’s digital financial future.

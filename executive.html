Executive Committee

Our dedicated Executive Committee drives the PSP Association’s strategic vision, representing the diverse interests of our global members.

President: Binay Khadka

CEO, Khalti by IME

Binay Khadka is a qualified Chartered Accountant and the Chief Executive Officer of Khalti, a leading digital wallet and payment service provider in Nepal. With a strong foundation in finance and strategic leadership, he has played a pivotal role in accelerating Khalti’s growth in the digital payments ecosystem. Prior to this role, he held key positions including Head of Banks and Corporate Relations at eSewa and Head of Digital Marketing at NIC Asia Bank, following his initial tenure as a Financial Analyst at the same institution. Mr. Khadka was the national topper in the Chartered Accountancy final examinations in 2017, receiving three gold medals from ICAN. His career reflects a deep commitment to advancing digital financial inclusion and fostering innovation in Nepal’s fintech landscape.

Member: Hemant Tandon Chhetri

CEO, Hamro Pay

Hemant Tandon Chhetri, the CEO of Hamro Pay, has a diverse professional background. He began as a professional graphic designer before transitioning to the banking sector, where he held leadership roles like Head of Legal, Compliance, and Recovery at Samriddhi Finance Limited, and Chief Legal Officer at NIC Asia Microfinance Limited. Academically, he holds an M.Phil. in Management (Human Resources), an MBS with a focus on Marketing Research and Global Marketing from T.U., and an LLB with an advocate license, making him a recognized banking/corporate advocate. He is currently pursuing a Master's in Mass Communication and Journalism at Tribhuvan University and also lectures in Management at Himalayan White House College and Pushpalal Memorial College, in addition to leading the Head of Research Department and Thesis Supervisor for MBS at Samata College.In addition to his other roles, he contributes to Nepalese literature as a writer and editor. His works, published by Mega Publication and Research Centre, include "District Profile of Nepal," "National Profile of Nepal," "Municipality Profile of Nepal," and "National Policies of Nepal."

Member: Pranaya Rajbhandari

CEO, MOCO

Pranaya Rajbhandari is the Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of FOCUSONE Payment Solutions, the company behind Nepal's innovative digital wallet platform, MOCO. Under his leadership, MOCO has introduced Nepal’s first unified QR code system, enabling seamless cross-border payments through global networks like Alipay+, UnionPay, Visa, and NepalPay. This initiative has positioned MOCO as a key player in Nepal’s digital payments ecosystem, facilitating cardless transactions and empowering businesses to engage with international customers. Mr. Rajbhandari is also a founding member of the PSP Association Nepal, contributing to the development of regulatory frameworks and industry standards that support the growth of digital financial services in the country.
